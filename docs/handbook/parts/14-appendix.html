Appendix A. Glossary

TermMeaning
MemberEvery account’s default workspace (internal role fan).
WorkspaceThe active role of an account: Member, Artist, Venue or Organizer. Only approved roles can be active.
ProviderAn artist, band, DJ, venue or lesson teacher who receives booking requests.
HostThe account that created an event (private or public). For public events the host is an approved organizer.
BookingOne agreement between a customer and one provider, with its own deadline, price lines and payment state.
RequestA booking seen from the provider’s side.
QuoteAn itemised, versioned price offer from a provider, valid until a date.
Cost sharingA private event setting where each accepted guest pays a contribution to receive a ticket.
Action queueThe list of items that need the user’s decision (attention banner and dashboard).
Would book againThe musician score: share of verified reviews that recommend booking the artist again.
EASExpo Application Services: cloud builds, store submission and over-the-air updates.
ConnectStripe’s marketplace product for paying out providers.

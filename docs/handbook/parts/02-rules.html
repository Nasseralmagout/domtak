2. Product rules, roles and permissions

The rules below are product requirements. The front-end already follows them. The backend must enforce every one of them independently: a client can be modified, so no rule may depend on the app hiding a button.

2.1 Accounts and workspaces

  • Every account starts as a Member (internal role fan). The member workspace is always available.
  • Three professional roles need a reviewed application: Artist (musician: solo artist, band or DJ), Venue (venue) and Organizer (organizer). Application states are pending, info (more information requested, with the exact missing items), approved and rejected (with a reason). Submitting an application never grants access by itself.
  • One account can hold several approved workspaces (approvedRoles). Exactly one is active at a time (role). Switching to a role that is not approved is refused.
  • Staff (admin, reviewer, support, finance, moderator) are not app roles. They are separate staff permissions on top of an account and are checked on every admin endpoint (section 5.4). DECISION The reviewer, support and finance authority hierarchy is open (blueprint F03, F24–F27).

2.4 Blueprint rules that the backend must guarantee

  1. A member cannot publish a public event without organizer approval.
  2. A professional application status is visible and does not grant access before approval.
  3. Every provider booking has its own response deadline and its own financial record. Artist and venue bookings for the same event are independent.
  4. The provider has 24 hours to respond from the moment a valid request reaches them. A reminder goes out after 12 hours, only while the request is still awaiting a response.
  5. An unanswered request expires at 24 hours. Expiry starts the release or refund; it never claims the release is already complete.
  6. Provider acceptance alone does not confirm a booking. A booking is confirmed only when the payment is verified.
  7. Declining one provider request does not cancel other confirmed bookings of the same event. Cancelling an event flags linked bookings for the cancellation process; it does not cancel them automatically.
  8. Agreed terms (price lines, date, quote version) are stored on the booking and stay unchanged when the provider later changes their prices.
  9. A payment retry happens on the same booking. It never creates a second booking or a second charge.
  10. Ticket validation refuses wrong-event, cancelled, refunded and already-used tickets, and shows when an already-used ticket was checked in.
  11. A private-event guest ticket (cost sharing) is issued only after the contribution payment succeeds. A failed payment never issues a ticket.
  12. Support cannot promise a refund before an authorized decision. A support case cannot be closed while a linked refund or release is still pending.
  13. Dashboard actions respect ownership and role permissions. Financial totals separate booking value, fees, refunds and provider payouts.
  14. Empty states show the real absence of activity. No fabricated numbers or results.
  15. Communities are created by the platform only in V1. Blocking someone also ends the friendship and declines pending friend requests. A reported account never learns who reported it.

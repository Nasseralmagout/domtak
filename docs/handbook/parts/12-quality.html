12.2 Acceptance criteria (from the blueprint)

CriterionHow to test
A member cannot publish a public event without organizer approval.API: publish as member, artist, venue and unapproved organizer → refused; approved organizer → allowed.
Application status is visible and does not grant premature access.Pending, info and rejected applicants get ROLE_NOT_APPROVED on professional endpoints.
Every provider booking has its own deadline and financial record.Event with artist and venue bookings: decline one, the other stays unchanged.
Reminder after 12 h only while awaiting a response.Clock-controlled job test: answered at 11 h → no reminder.
Expiry at 24 h without claiming the release is complete.Status expired, payment release_pending until the webhook.
Acceptance alone does not confirm a booking if payment fails.Accept + failed capture → payment_failed, not confirmed.
Rejecting one request does not cancel unrelated confirmed bookings.As above, plus event cancellation flags bookings instead of cancelling them.
Agreed terms remain after profile prices change.Change provider fee after booking → booking charges unchanged.
Ticket validation rejects wrong-event, cancelled, refunded and used tickets.Scan each case; used ticket shows the first check-in time.
Dashboard actions respect ownership and role permissions.Access other users’ resources → 403/404.
Financial totals separate booking value, fees, refunds and payouts.Ledger totals match Stripe test data.
Empty states show real absence of activity.New account: every list shows its empty state, no sample content.
All flows work in English on every platform.E2E suite passes on Android, iOS and web.

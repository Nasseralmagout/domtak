6. Data model

The front-end’s domain types (mobile/src/data/types.ts and the interfaces in src/store/useApp.ts) are the contract. The tables below are the recommended PostgreSQL schema behind them. Table names are snake_case; the API returns camelCase JSON matching the TypeScript types.

Conventions. Primary keys are UUIDs. Every table has created_at and updated_at. Money is stored as integer cents with a currency (EUR), never as floating point; the app formats cents to euro. Timestamps are timestamptz in UTC; the app shows Europe/Berlin time. Soft-delete only where retention requires it (bookings, payments, tickets); otherwise delete.
One booking, two views. The demo keeps the customer’s bookings (Booking) and the provider’s incoming requests (IncomingRequest) as separate sample lists. In the backend they are the same booking row, returned from the customer side by /v1/bookings and from the provider side by /v1/provider/requests. A provider’s counter offer becomes a quote revision on that booking.

The legacy status paid (payment verified, not yet confirmed) is kept for compatibility; new bookings move straight from accepted to confirmed when payment is verified. The client mapping is paymentAfter() in mobile/src/lib/booking.ts.

Quote-based bookings

quote requested→quote sent (v1)→accepted · payment→confirmed→completed
  • Requesting a quote charges nothing. The provider sends an itemised quote with a validity date; each revision increases the version and needs the customer’s acceptance again.
  • Accepting a valid quote moves the booking to accepted and starts the payment. An expired or unsent quote cannot be accepted. Declining ends the offer without a booking.
  • DECISION Quote validity length, the provider deadline for quotes, and whether customer acceptance replaces provider confirmation (blueprint F15–F16).

6.7 Pricing and scoring rules

RuleFormula (from mobile/src/lib)
CateringpricePerGuest × max(guests, minGuests), only if available and guests > 0
Securityceil(guests / guestsPerGuard) × pricePerGuard; mandatory when guests ≥ requiredFrom
Artist estimateBase = fee per hour × hours (no base price when the artist prices by quote); travel and equipment are separate lines; optional service fee line. The provider’s minimum hours (availability) are enforced on the request.
Lesson estimatePrice per session × sessions (its own price, never the performance fee); quote-only lessons have no price
Customer service fee5% of the booking total (demo rate) DECISION
Platform commission10% of each booking paid through the app, deducted from the provider’s payout (as stated in the Terms) DECISION final rate and who pays
